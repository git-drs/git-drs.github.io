The library

Seven ways to
meet your music.

Every screen below is shown twice: the dark version first, the light version right next to it. Scroll on, because they move at different speeds.

01

Library · Tracks

Every song,
instantly.

Your whole library in one clean, fast list. Each track shows its duration right on the thumbnail, with the title and artist underneath, and the song that's playing glows in Pulse red so you never lose your place.

On screen

  • Animated tab bar on top: Folders, Queue, Playlists, Albums
  • A search field that sits right beside the page title
  • Currently playing track highlighted in red
02

Library · Albums

Albums as a gallery.

Browse by album in a bold two-column grid. Big artwork, the album name, the artist and the number of songs on every tile. Video files get their own thumbnail too, so your whole collection looks alive.

On screen

  • Two-column grid of artwork cards
  • Artist and song count under every album
  • Mini player stays docked while you browse
03

Library · Playlists

Playlists, one tap away.

Start a new playlist with a single button. Your Favorites collection sits right underneath, marked with its heart and a live count of how many songs it holds.

On screen

  • 'Create your playlist' button at the top of the list
  • Favorites playlist with its heart and live song count
  • Playback controls remain one tap away at the bottom
04

Library · Inside a playlist

Dive into a playlist.

Open a playlist and the header melts into blurred artwork with a big title and song count. Hit Play all to start from the top, or change the sort order from the menu on the right.

On screen

  • Blurred artwork header with large title
  • Song count plus a one-tap 'Play all'
  • Sort menu ('By name') and an overflow menu
05

Library · Folders

Browse the way your files live.

The Folders tab turns your storage into a feed. Every folder gets its own row of swipeable thumbnails with a duration badge and a date, and 'More' opens the full folder.

On screen

  • One horizontal rail per folder: Music, Download, Drum Loops, Risers
  • Duration badge and date on each item
  • 'More' jumps straight into the whole folder
06

Library · Inside a folder

Open a folder.
Play all.

Inside a folder you get the same polished header treatment: the folder name, how many songs it holds, a Play all button and sorting, so a whole directory becomes a listening session in one tap.

On screen

  • Folder name with its song count (e.g. 124 songs)
  • 'Play all' and sort controls under the header
  • Back arrow and menu in the translucent top bar
07

Library · Queue

Take control of the queue.

See exactly what's coming up next. The Playback Queue shows how many tracks are lined up, lets you drag songs into a new order with the handles on the left, and has quick actions to add to or clear the queue.

On screen

  • 'Playback Queue' with a live track count
  • Drag handles on the left of every track to reorder
  • Add and clear buttons at the top right

Now playing

Your cover. Your shape.

01

Cover art · Circle

Round, like a record.

Your cover art spins into a perfect circle over a gradient pulled from the artwork itself. Heart a song with one tap, reach quick actions from the icons along the top, and scrub the seek bar with live timestamps.

On screen

  • Circular cover art, centred
  • Heart button next to the title
  • Shuffle, previous, play/pause, next, repeat
02

Cover art · Squared

A rounded square frame.

Prefer the classic album look? Switch the cover art shape to Square and the artwork sits in a soft-cornered tile. The colours still flow from the cover into the whole player background.

On screen

  • Rounded-square artwork
  • Gradient extracted from the album art
  • Same full set of transport controls
03

Cover art · Full art

Art, edge to edge.

Go all-in with Full art: the cover stretches across the top of the screen and fades into the controls. It's the most immersive way to watch a song play.

On screen

  • Cover fills the top of the screen
  • Title, artist and album in the fade
  • Seek bar with elapsed and total time

Navigation layout

Tabs up top.
Or thumbs down low.

Choose how you move around. The top layout uses animated flight tabs that glide between Tracks, Queue, Playlists and Albums. The bottom bar puts Tracks, Folders, Playlists and Albums right under your thumb.

A Top tabs

Animated flight tabs and a search field share the header, so more of the screen is yours.

B Bottom bar

Four icons under your thumb, the mini player sitting just above them.

08

Settings

Tune it until it's yours.

Settings are grouped into Appearance, Library and Playback, each as a tidy card with a toggle, a slider or a picker. Here is everything you can change.

Appearance

  • Dark ModeSwitch between dark and light theme
  • Navigation LayoutTop tabs with animated flight tabs, or a bottom bar
  • Cover Art ShapeCircle, square or full art
  • Auto GradientExtract colours from album art
  • Background BlurBlur album art behind the player, with an intensity slider

Library

  • Folder FilterOnly show songs from the folders you pick

Playback

  • Gapless PlaybackNo silence between tracks
  • CrossfadeBlend one track into the next
  • Volume NormalizationEven volume across tracks
  • Pause on Audio LossPause automatically when audio output drops
